Truth Labeler

Load a clip, then label while it plays. Clips built from photo sequences show one photo per second. Keys: Space play/pause, ←/→ previous/next photo, ,/. ±0.1 s, E add entrance, T tactile start/end, O obstacle start/end. Labels are recorded as whole photo numbers. Keys are ignored while a text box, number box or dropdown has focus. Labeling time is counted while a clip is loaded and this tab is visible. Idle time (no key, mouse, wheel or seek for 60 s) is not counted.
